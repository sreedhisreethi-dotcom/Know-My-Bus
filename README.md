
## Know My Bus – AI-Based Bus Route Finding System

**Know My Bus** is an AI-powered web application designed to help passengers easily find suitable bus services based on their **boarding location, destination, and preferred boarding time**.

The project focuses on solving a common problem faced by people who are unfamiliar with local bus routes and timings. Instead of depending on information available only at the bus stand or asking others for directions, users can enter their requirements into the website and view the **next available buses, bus type, departure time, route, stops, and prototype ticket fare**.

The current prototype focuses on **Pollachi Bus Stand** and includes selected routes from Pollachi to **Coimbatore, Tiruppur, and Ooty**, along with government and private bus options. The system also considers the operating cycle of buses to identify upcoming available services.

The AI component helps interpret user requirements and recommends the most suitable available bus based on the selected route and boarding time. The system is designed to make public-transport information more accessible, especially for students, youngsters, visitors, and people who are unfamiliar with local bus services.

The prototype also includes **English and Tamil language support** and provides a visual representation of the selected bus route and stops.

**Note:** The current version uses prototype/demo bus schedules and fares for demonstration purposes. It is not connected to official real-time government transport data.
